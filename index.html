06 / FAQ

Good to know.

How do I request a quote?

Tell us the service, property type, area or postal code and priority rooms.

What will my cleaning include?

We discuss the tasks and rooms you want covered and agree on the scope before booking.

Can I request a specific date?

Yes. Share your preferred date or time window and we’ll discuss availability.

Can I tell you about pets or product preferences?

Yes. Tell us about pets, access arrangements, fragrances or products you’d like to discuss.

When should I plan a move-out clean?

Share your handover date and when rooms will be clear of boxes and furniture.

What access is needed for windows?

Tell us which panes need cleaning, whether they’re inside or outside and how they can be accessed.

Is steam cleaning suitable for my surface?

Suitability depends on the surface or material. Describe what needs cleaning and share any care instructions.

How can I contact OCDbrothers?

Use WhatsApp, phone or email. See contact options.
