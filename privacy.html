Your enquiry information.

How information you share when contacting OCDbrothers is used.

What you share

When you contact us, you may share your name, contact details, general location, property type and cleaning requirements. An area or postal code is enough to start an enquiry.

How it is used

We use enquiry details to respond, discuss a quote and arrange an appointment if you decide to book. Avoid sending access codes or payment information with your first message.

Contact channels

Email and WhatsApp are handled by their respective providers. If the contact page offers a Tally form, Tally processes submissions and an authorised Google Sheets integration may store enquiries for follow-up.
